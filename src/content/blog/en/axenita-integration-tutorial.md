You can now use DocNote directly from your Axenita EHR. Open DocNote from the patient file, generate your report and send it straight into Axenita, with no copy-pasting and no change to your routine. The video (in French) shows how to request the connection, then the everyday workflow. Each step is described below.

## Step 1: request the connection

The first time you sign in to DocNote, open **Settings**. In the section for your electronic health record, select **Axenita** and click **Request connection**. The DocNote and Axenita teams take care of the activation and confirm once it is live.

## Step 2: use DocNote from the patient file

Once the connection is confirmed, keep working in Axenita as usual.

1. **Open DocNote from Axenita.** In the patient file, click "External call", the icon to the right of the printer, then select DocNote. The app opens automatically.
2. **Create the consultation.** Start a new consultation: the patient's last name, first name and date of birth are already filled in on the new report.
3. **Record or dictate** your consultation as you normally would, then generate the report.
4. **Send it to the patient file.** When the report is finished, send it directly to the patient's file in Axenita. Reports created in the last seven days can be found and sent the same way.

## Step 3: add the report to the consultation

Close DocNote and return to Axenita. In the patient file, click the double-arrow icon: the content sent from DocNote appears there. Select the matching consultation, click **Replace**, then confirm.

The report is now part of the patient's record in Axenita.

## Why this approach

The Axenita integration is built on the FHIR standard: the report travels as a structured document rather than pasted text, and the patient's identity travels with it. For the doctor, the visible result is even simpler: one click to open DocNote, one click to send, nothing to retype.
